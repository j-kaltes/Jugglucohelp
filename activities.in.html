Activities

Let Juggluco retrieve recorded activities from Health Connect. To make use of this, you should configure other apps to send activities to Health Connect, for example in Samsung Health, Strava or Garmin Connect.

Create Amounts from activities: Use the configuration under Labels to create for each activity an amount under a label.

Labels: In a list of all activity types Health Connect differentiates, you can set for each activity with which label of Juggluco they should be associated and what measure of the activity should be displayed under that label. If you don’t select anything, “Activity” will the the label and duration the measure.

Retrieve from: Starting with what date and time should activities be retrieved from Health Connect. On older phones this will be restricted to activities not older than 30 days before Juggluco received Health Connect permission.

Save: Save the setting changes.

Retrieve: Retrieve data now from Health Connect.

Automatic retrieval: Schedule Juggluco to regularly retrieve data from Health Connect.

Saved activities: List of activities saved. If “Create Amounts from activities” was set, these activities can also be viewed by long pressing the amount followed by “Activity”
