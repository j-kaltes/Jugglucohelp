CAMERA permission

To use a Sibionics, Aidex X, Dexcom, Accu-Chek SmartGuide and Caresens Air sensor, you need to scan a data matrix on the package or applicator making use of the camera of the phone. For this Juggluco can make use of either Google Play services in which case Juggluco doesn’t need camera permission or Juggluco makes use of zXing in which case Juggluco needs camera permission.
