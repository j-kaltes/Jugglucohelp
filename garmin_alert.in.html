Alert

Sound: turn on sound for alarms (only available on a few watches).

Vibrate: turn on vibration for this alarm.

Duration: when an alarm occurs, how long should the watch vibrate or generate sound?

Minutes deactivated: after the alarm went off, how long should it wait until the alarm is generated again in case the condition continues. For example when a low glucose alarm went off at 7:30 and 20 minutes is specified, and the glucose level stays low, the alarm is only generated again after 7:50.
