Location permission

Devices running Android 6 to 11 need location permission to find Bluetooth devices. This scanning for Bluetooth devices is needed for Aidex X, Accu-Check SmartGuide, CareSens Air and Dexcom G7 and Sibionics sensors and to receive glucose measurement from finger-stick glucose meters via Bluetooth.

Scanning for Bluetooth devices happens regularly when connecting and reconnecting to Bluetooth devices, it doesn’t happen only one time.

On Android 10 and Android 11 also location background permission is needed to enable this scanning for devices when Juggluco is in the background.

To allow Android to connect to these Bluetooth devices in the background, you need to select “Allow all the time” when giving Juggluco location permission. “Using the app” means having the app in the foreground.

This location permission is only used for finding Bluetooth devices and not for anything else.
