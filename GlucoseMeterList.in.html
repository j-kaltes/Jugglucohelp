Glucose meter list

Juggluco can receive blood glucose test measurements via Bluetooth from Contour Next ONE glucose meters. Scan the data matrix on the glucose meter with left menu→Photo. A screen will be presented on which you have to select the label for blood glucose and specify from what date and time you will receive glucose measurements.

The first time you need to bring the glucosemeter in PAIRING mode, for example by long pressing the button of the powered off meter until a blue light blinks.

Juggluco shows here the glucose meters already used with Juggluco. If you uncheck “Active” Juggluco will no longer connect to them.
