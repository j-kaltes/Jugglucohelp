Glucose meter list

Juggluco can receive blood glucose test measurements via Bluetooth from some blood glucose meters.

If you have a Contour Next ONE, you can add it to Juggluco by scanning the data matrix on the glucose meter with left menu→Photo.

For other meters, you need to press “Find devices” and press the device when it is found.

A screen will be presented on which you have to select the label for blood glucose and specify from what date and time you will receive glucose measurements.

The first time you need to bring the glucosemeter in PAIRING mode, for example by long pressing the button of the powered off meter until a blue light blinks.


Juggluco shows here the glucose meters already used with Juggluco.

For every glucose meter, the device name and address are shown — these are the same values you'll see under "Connected devices" in Android's Settings.

Last: time of last glucose value received from this glucose meter.

(Dis)Connected: last time (dis)connected

No new data: At this time there was a successful exchange with the glucose meter, but there was no new data;

New data: received new data at that time.

Active: If you uncheck “Active” Juggluco will no longer connect to the glucose meter.
